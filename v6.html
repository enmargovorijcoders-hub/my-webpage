Unlocking Your Phones Bootloader:

Depending on your model, there are just a couple of easy steps to ROMing your phone:

1. Unlocking your Bootloader: Most of the time its just as easy as going to about clicking your biuld number until it says "You Are a Developer!" toggleing OEM Unlocking and USB Debugging, then on your computer with the phone conected and USB debuging enabled (Click Here for a guide on how to enable and install ADB on your Mac/Linux/Windows) if on Windows you have to get drivers for your device by looking it up on google and installing those drivers. Once you have adb ready on you Windows pc type: "adb reboot bootloader" on Mac/Linux type: "./adb reboot bootloader" then when you are in fastboot it should say it on the screen type on Windows: "fastboot oem unlock" or "fastboot flashing unlock" on Macos/Linux: "./fastboot oem unlock" or "fastboot flashing unlock" press Vol+ on your device and done your bootloader is unlocked.

Warning this will delete all of you data on the device!
